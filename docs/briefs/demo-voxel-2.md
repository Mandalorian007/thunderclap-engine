## Goal

`demos/voxel` becomes the reference for "a voxel game on the engine": the game keeps its blocks, its noise, its textures and its controls; the mesher, the grid, the worker bridge, the chunk drawable with culling, the DDA pick and the uv wrap come from `@thunderclap/engine/voxel`. The demo shrinks (target: `mesher.worker.ts` and the chunk bookkeeping gone, `world.ts` holding generation and block rules only), the frame cost and the draw-call count do not rise, and the open "+z riser draws as a triangle" item is either fixed by the move or pinned to a specific engine line and reported as a wish.

STATUS row, *done when*:

- **Migration**: `demos/voxel/mesher.worker.ts` is deleted or reduced to a one-line `createVoxelMesherWorker()` entry from the engine recipe (say which the README recipe allows); `world.ts` keeps generation (noise, caves, trees, glowstone) and block rules (`layerOf`, hardness, placeable) and writes into `createVoxelGrid`; chunk meshes, slack and culling come from `createVoxelChunks` (state `growths` stays 0 after place + break, `drawCalls` from the spawn view under 200 as today); break and place use `raycastVoxels`; the block material uses the `nearest-mip-repeat` sampler and `VOXEL_WGSL`'s `voxel_layer`/`voxel_uv_wrap` (the hand-wrap and the layer decode leave the demo's WGSL); `Water` is placed with `origin` at sea level and the world is no longer centred on the origin for its sake (the spawn and the noise may move; the spec's steady-state expectations update with them); `Sky` uses `ambientFloor` for the night.
